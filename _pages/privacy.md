# Privacy and visitor statistics

Updated 12 November 2026.

This is Inam Ullah Khan’s academic and educational website. Its public pages are hosted by GitHub Pages.

## Aggregate page-view counter

From 12 November 2026, a shared counter records aggregate page views across this portfolio and Knowledge & Opportunity to understand and improve these resources. It does not use tracking cookies, persistent visitor IDs or fingerprinting. It sends only a page path; the hosting network supplies an approximate country. It does not send referring websites or advertising labels.

Choose **Pause counting** in the counter, or **Opt out** in the notice, to stop counting for this browser. Previous refusals and Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signals are respected. A browser preference remembers your choice; it is not a visitor identifier. If storage is unavailable, the choice applies to this page only.

Only daily aggregate counts are stored in the analytics database, without raw IP addresses, names, account details, full URLs, query strings or individual visit records. Network addresses are briefly used in memory for abuse control. Hosting providers separately process request information to deliver and secure their services. Counts are used to improve the website, not for advertising or identifying readers.

The public counter combines the portfolio and Knowledge & Opportunity. It refreshes every 30 seconds while the page is visible; refreshing totals does not add a view. These are **page views, not unique people**. Reloads count again. Country estimates can reflect a VPN, and unknown locations are shown separately. Opt-outs, blocked scripts, bots and network failures reduce coverage.

Historical totals are preserved. Before 12 November 2026, only visits that allowed statistics were recorded. Unrecorded earlier traffic cannot be reconstructed. Older detailed page and referrer reports remain restricted to the publisher.

## Hosting and external content

GitHub may collect technical information, including IP addresses, when serving this website. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The visitor-insights service is delivered using OpenAI’s Sites infrastructure; see [OpenAI’s Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/).

Some existing research pages use external mathematical rendering or other embedded resources. Visiting an external link takes you to that service, which has its own privacy practices. Scholarship and job applications are handled by the linked organisations, not collected on this website.

## Advertising

Google AdSense code is installed on this public website using the publisher account `ca-pub-4993619915688606`. Installing the code supports Google’s site verification and advertising integration; it does not establish that Google has approved the site or that advertisements are being served.

When this code loads, your browser connects to Google. Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser information, and may use cookies or similar technologies according to the service, settings and applicable consent choices. See [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The **Pause counting** control applies only to this website’s aggregate page-view counter. It does not set Google advertising preferences. Where Google displays a consent message, use its consent and manage-options controls for Google advertising choices. You can also review your preferences in [Google’s My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).

## Public readership total

The shared footer shows all recorded page views, today, the last seven days, country totals and flags. It stores no individual visitor history. Counts refresh automatically and a timestamp shows the last successful retrieval; failed requests display an error rather than a fabricated zero. Reading the total does not itself record a page view. The visible public page records at most one view per document and page path; dashboard and API routes are excluded.
