## MIBTP

Birmingham’s competitive MIBTP route offers four-year training. The university states that tuition, including international tuition, is covered. Its published stipend is **£21,805 for 2026/27**; a 2027/28 figure is not confirmed here. International awards are limited by programme rules.

Applications close **23 November 2026** for October 2027 entry. Use the project-specific Birmingham link, identify the project and supervisor, and complete the equality-monitoring form. Birmingham permits applications to up to five programmes, with one project per programme and separate applications. Individual project requirements still apply; any conflicting nationality label is flagged on its card.

Sources: [Birmingham MIBTP funding, projects and applications](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/about/college-of-life-and-environmental-sciences/midlands-integrative-biosciences-training-partnership) · [MIBTP applicant guidance](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/cross_fac/mibtp/).

## CENTA

The project-based competition closes **6 January 2027 at 23:59 GMT**, for autumn 2027 entry. Choose **one Birmingham project**. Apply through the university with the CENTA form, CV, referees and equality-monitoring information.

Awards last **3.5 years**, with a stipend, Home-rate tuition and an **£8,000 research-training grant**. The published stipend is **£21,805 for 2026/27**, not a confirmed 2027/28 rate. International applicants can compete, within a 30% cohort cap, but must confirm coverage of any overseas tuition shortfall. Visa and relocation expenses are not covered.

Academic eligibility is normally a UK 2:1 bachelor’s or a pass at Master’s level, or equivalent. Check project-specific requirements. The separate CSOS application route is already closed.

Sources: [CENTA application rules](https://centa.ac.uk/apply/) · [CENTA funding terms](https://centa.ac.uk/apply/centa-studentship-information/).

## HPRU genomics

The three Birmingham–UKHSA projects are **Home-fee-only**, with a stated **£21,805 stipend**. Email a two-page CV and cover letter to the address on Birmingham’s page by **23 November 2026**, identifying the project(s). Confirm duration and tuition coverage directly.

Source: [Birmingham’s health-protection PhD opportunities](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/research/centres-institutes/microbiology-and-infection/phd-opportunities).

## Individual project awards

Read each card’s fee-status conditions carefully. “International applicants welcome” does not by itself establish that overseas tuition is covered. Some adverts omit the stipend or funding duration; these are left unspecified. The Centre-UB reading project has a published closing time of **17:00 GMT on 30 November 2026** ([programme advert](https://www.centre-ub.org/studentships/new-opportunities/)).

Rolling projects can be filled before an advertising end date. The thermal-battery and battery-recycling adverts contain date discrepancies and need confirmation before an application. A red line on the subject pages marks a verified dated deadline within seven days; deadlines become “passed” automatically.
