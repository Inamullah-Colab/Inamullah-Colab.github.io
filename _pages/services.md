I am open to selected collaborations and consulting work at the intersection of **biomedical data**, **interpretable machine learning**, **retinal imaging**, and **mathematical modelling**.

This page is intended as a clear entry point for research groups, academic collaborators, clinicians, startups, and technical teams who may want support on scientifically rigorous data-driven projects.

## Areas I Can Help With

- **Biomedical data analysis** for multimodal datasets involving imaging, clinical variables, omics, or genetics
- **Interpretable machine learning** workflows where explanation and model transparency matter
- **Statistical modelling** including regression, multivariate methods, mediation, and causal analysis
- **Retinal imaging and oculomics** projects involving feature extraction, biological interpretation, or downstream modelling
- **Scientific computing and research pipelines** for reproducible analysis, benchmarking, and result reporting
- **Research writing and technical review** for methods sections, mathematical explanations, and analytical framing

## Typical Collaboration Formats

- Short technical consultation
- Research collaboration
- Analytical support for a specific dataset
- Method development for an ongoing scientific project
- Independent review of modelling strategy or analytical design
- Scientific content or technical article development

## What I Prioritise

I am especially interested in work that values:

- interpretability over black-box claims
- methodological clarity
- strong quantitative reasoning
- reproducible workflows
- biomedical relevance

## Good Fit Projects

Examples of projects that are likely to be a good fit:

- retinal imaging linked with systemic or molecular traits
- multimodal biomedical integration
- causal or mediation-oriented analysis
- mathematically grounded machine learning for healthcare
- signal, wave, or geometry-based scientific modelling
