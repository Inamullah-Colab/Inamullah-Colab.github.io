What is behind this demo?+

The grading study

10,000 EyePACS training images, 2,910 APTOS validation images and 1,636 Messidor-2 test images, matched across retinal evidence streams.

The frozen ResNet-152 checkpoint is used for uploads. An audit found different X3 preprocessing in the previous training and external-evaluation scripts. Historical scores do not establish performance for this upload pipeline; consistent reevaluation is required.

The language assistant

Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct plans optional explanation details. Supplied facts and scope guards control the response. The standalone LLM routed 24/36 development examples correctly; the guarded flow passed 36/36. This is not an independent clinical benchmark.

Uploaded photographs run through the original X1–X4 feature extractors and frozen ResNet-152 grader. The assistant explains the server-held prediction. Example contexts remain clearly illustrative. No clinical validation or treatment decisions are provided.
