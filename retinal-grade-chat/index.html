Processing and privacy information

JPG/PNG images: maximum 10 MB and 30 megapixels. Image analysis uses the connected research server and can take several minutes. Temporary image files are removed after processing; result images remain in memory for 30 minutes. Colab JSON reports: maximum 20 MB, displayed only in your browser without being sent to the model server. Saved report values are not independently verified by this page.

A SIMPLE GUIDE TO THE FOUR INPUTS

Four ways of looking at the same retina

X1–X4 are names for four types of information. They help the grader consider more than the photograph alone.

X1

The vessel map

Think of the blood vessels as a road network. X1 describes how its thin centerlines connect, branch and end.

You can see: vessel overlays, skeletons and network counts.
X2

Visual clues

X2 summarizes weak image cues that may help grading. A separate Grad-CAM picture shows where the image model focused.

A colored area is a clue or model attribution, not a confirmed lesion.
X3

Learned image patterns

An encoder trained using lesion-focused learning turns the image into a numerical description for the grader.

These numbers do not provide lesion locations or counts.
X4

Vessel measurements

X4 measures traits such as vessel width, density, bending and pattern complexity, including different vessel types and regions.

72 measurements. Their values alone are not a healthy/unhealthy verdict.

Research methods, capabilities and limitations +

The grading model

The frozen ResNet-152 fusion model uses the photograph and X1–X4. The research cohort includes 10,000 EyePACS training images, 2,910 APTOS validation images and 1,636 Messidor-2 test images.

An audit found inconsistent X3 preprocessing in the historical external evaluation. Those scores require reevaluation and do not establish clinical performance for this upload pipeline.

The language assistant

Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct selects cited facts from a curated library of 26 topics and uses the current report’s measured values. This is retrieval augmentation, with no new model-weight training.

It does not diagnose systemic disease, confirm lesions, prescribe treatment or retrieve patient history. Clinician assessments are not recorded in this workspace.
