What is behind this demo?+

The grading study

10,000 EyePACS training images, 2,910 APTOS validation images and 1,636 Messidor-2 test images, matched across retinal evidence streams.

The APTOS-selected ResNet-152 model achieved Messidor QWK 0.562 and detected 7 of 30 grade-4 cases. These research results do not establish clinical readiness.

The language assistant

Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct plans optional explanation details. Supplied facts and scope guards control the response. The standalone LLM routed 24/36 development examples correctly; the guarded flow passed 36/36. This is not an independent clinical benchmark.

The live demo explains illustrative contexts. It does not run the image grader on an uploaded scan.
