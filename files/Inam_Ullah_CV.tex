\section*{Professional Profile}
Researcher in oculomics, interpretable machine learning, and multimodal biomedical data integration. My work studies how retinal microvascular structure can serve as a non-invasive marker of systemic biology by linking imaging, lipidomics, genetics, and clinical phenotypes. I develop mathematically grounded and biologically interpretable methods for fragmented and high-dimensional health data, with emphasis on causal modelling, mediation analysis, pathway-aware representation, and clinically relevant biomarker discovery.

\section*{Core Research Areas}
