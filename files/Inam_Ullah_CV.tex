\section*{Professional Profile}
PhD researcher working at the intersection of oculomics, interpretable machine learning, causal modelling, and multimodal biomedical data integration. My research studies how retinal microvascular structure can act as a non-invasive marker of systemic biology by linking imaging, lipidomics, genetics, and clinical phenotypes. I build mathematically grounded and clinically interpretable methods for high-dimensional health data, with particular interest in pathway-aware modelling, mediation analysis, multimodal integration, and robust translational AI.

\section*{Research Interests}
