01 / CAUSAL MODELLING

How do we learn from fragmented data?

RetiSEM

Biomedical measurements are often incomplete across datasets. RetiSEM studies how to recover interpretable causal structure from fragmented, multimodal evidence.

02 / MULTI-OMICS

What connects the retina and lipidome?

Oculomics × Lipidomics

Can the eye reveal molecular signals elsewhere in the body? This study characterises cross-sectional associations between retinal vascular traits and lipidomic profiles in a generally healthy biobank population.

Retinal phenotyping · Lipidomics · Statistics

How can retinal AI explain its evidence?

04 / RESEARCH SOFTWARE

How do we make model results inspectable?

RetinalInsight

A retinal grading workspace that brings the model result together with vessel morphology, weak lesion evidence, Grad-CAM attention, vascular biomarkers and cited explanations.

Vessel structure · Lesion-evidence cues · XAI · Biomarkers

Built on AutoMorph, Huang and colleagues’ lesion-based contrastive learning, and Grad-CAM. Research prototype requiring clinical review.

The person behind the research

An interdisciplinary
path to healthcare AI.

Read more about me →

I began my professional journey in civil engineering as an associate engineer, learning to turn complex problems into practical solutions. Training in physics and mathematics gave me a language for understanding systems; computer science gave me the tools to model them.

Today, these perspectives come together in my PhD research. I develop machine learning and statistical methods that integrate retinal imaging with molecular and clinical data, seeking interpretable insights into human health. Engineering keeps my questions grounded in practical constraints; mathematical reasoning helps me examine the assumptions behind the answers.

Engineering practice → Mathematical reasoning → Computational health research
