03 Nov 2026Research software · RetinalInsight

Beyond a grade: bringing the evidence into view.

A retinal photograph holds more than a single score. RetinalInsight brings vessel structure, visual clues, model attention and vascular measurements into one research workspace—so the result can be explored, questioned and understood.

Making the reasoning visible. The photograph is considered alongside a vessel map (X1), weak visual-evidence cues (X2), lesion-trained image features (X3) and vessel biomarkers (X4). A separate Grad-CAM view shows where the image model focused; the assistant helps explain the measured results with cited context.

From methods to an inspectable workspace. Built on AutoMorph, Huang and Li Lin’s lesion-based contrastive-learning work, and Grad-CAM, the prototype connects established research methods with an accessible interface. Its next chapter is careful evaluation and refinement of the explanations.
