## Software engineering

**Czechia, multiple locations · Job 200062893 · Posted 10 November 2026**

- **Requirements in brief:** a relevant bachelor's degree; software engineering with object-oriented languages; cloud-platform experience; networking and distributed-systems troubleshooting; SQL-like querying and infrastructure as code. Security screening applies.
