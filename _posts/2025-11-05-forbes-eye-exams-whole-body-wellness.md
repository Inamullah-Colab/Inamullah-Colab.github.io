Forbes published a feature on **November 5, 2025** titled
["How Artificial Intelligence Makes Eye Exams a Gateway to Whole-Body Wellness"](https://www.forbes.com/sites/williamhaseltine/2025/11/05/how-artificial-intelligence-makes-eye-exams-a-gateway-to-whole-body-wellness/).

The article features the broader idea at the center of our paper:
**"The Eye as a Window to Systemic Health: A Survey of Retinal Imaging from Classical Techniques to Oculomics."**

Its main message is that **AI-enabled eye exams can act as a gateway to whole-body wellness**. That framing closely matches the argument developed in our survey: retinal imaging is not limited to ocular assessment, but can reveal systemic cardiovascular, metabolic, and neurological signals through a non-invasive window into human health.

This coverage strengthens the broader clinical and translational relevance of oculomics and supports the view that retinal biomarkers, when combined with artificial intelligence, can contribute meaningfully to whole-body health assessment.
