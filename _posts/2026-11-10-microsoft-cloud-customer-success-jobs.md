## Solution Area Specialist (Cloud & AI)

**United Kingdom and Switzerland, multiple locations · Job 200064661 · Posted 9 November 2026**

Lead cloud and AI sales opportunities for Japan-headquartered organisations operating across Europe, the Middle East and Africa. The work involves executive relationships, account planning and coordinating specialists around customer needs.

- **Requirements in brief:** demonstrated enterprise technology sales, familiarity with major cloud platforms, practical understanding of AI use cases, senior stakeholder relationships and experience coordinating complex sales opportunities.
- **Work arrangement:** two office days per week; travel of 25–50%.
- **Advertised annual base pay:** £95,300–£136,100 in the UK; CHF 167,100–277,600 in Switzerland. These are separate country ranges; other compensation may apply.

## Principal Customer Success Account Manager – Public Sector

**London, United Kingdom · Job 200063241 · Posted 10 November 2026**

Coordinate long-term customer relationships and delivery plans across Microsoft's products and services. The position focuses on helping customers realise business value and aligning executives, technical teams and partners.

- **Requirements in brief:** a relevant bachelor's or master's degree with substantial customer success, consulting, solution delivery or portfolio experience, or equivalent experience. Relevant Microsoft, service-management, project-management or change-management certifications are preferred.
- **Work arrangement:** three office days per week; travel of 50–75%.
- **Advertised annual UK base pay:** £79,700–£133,900. Other compensation may apply.
- **Detail to clarify:** the official title says “Public Sector”, while its overview refers to a Media, Communications and Sports team. Confirm the customer portfolio with the hiring team.
