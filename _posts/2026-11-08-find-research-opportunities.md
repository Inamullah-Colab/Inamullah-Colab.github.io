[EURAXESS](https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/jobs) brings together research jobs, funding opportunities and hosting offers. It is a useful starting point for exploring academic and research careers.

## Search for the work you want to do

Begin with a few specific research terms. For example, a biomedical computing search might use “medical imaging”, “health data” or “machine learning”. Then read the full advert rather than judging fit from the job title alone.

For each promising role, write down the research question, essential skills, institution, contract details, deadline and official application link.

## Make your evidence easy to assess

Choose one concrete example for each essential criterion. A code repository, research result or teaching example is more informative when you explain your own contribution and how the work was evaluated.

Tailor the opening of your application to the project. Keep a short submission checklist for references, representative publications and supporting documents.

## Return to the employer’s advert

Before submitting, check the employer’s official page for current details and use its stated application route. Save the advert with your application materials.

**Source:** [EURAXESS — Jobs & Opportunities](https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/jobs), checked 8 November 2026. The application checklist is editorial guidance.

*This is a search guide, not an individual vacancy announcement.*
