## Business Data Scientist, AI Foundations

**San Jose, California · Google experience level: Mid**

Build models, forecasts and measurement frameworks for the AI Foundations Finance team. The work links engineering investment and AI infrastructure with business decisions, including assessing impact and returns.

- **Minimum background:** a quantitative master's degree or equivalent practical experience; three years working with AI products, technologies or infrastructure; statistical modelling, causal inference, experimentation or A/B testing; and regression-based forecasting.
- **Useful additional experience:** dashboards, data visualisation and applied analytics for engineering or business problems.
- **Advertised US pay range:** USD 138,000–197,000, plus a 15% bonus target, equity and benefits under the employer's terms.
- **Interview note:** the advert says in-person interviews are required in most instances.

## Senior Software Engineer, Discover Feed Recommendations

**Mountain View, California · Google experience level: Mid**

Develop the systems that help new content reach suitable audiences in Google Discover. The work includes machine-learning experiments, model deployment and the infrastructure supporting recommendation and retrieval.

- **Minimum background:** a bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience; five years developing software; three years in end-to-end machine learning; and three years building production recommendation models, with architecture experience. Personalisation and integration of generative-AI tools are also listed.
- **Preferred areas:** postgraduate study, algorithms, reinforcement learning or bandit methods, and recommendation-system evaluation.
- **Advertised US pay range:** USD 174,000–252,000, plus a 15% bonus target, equity and benefits under the employer's terms.
- **Interview note:** the advert says in-person interviews are required in most instances.

[Read the official advert and apply →](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/84408490079986374-senior-software-engineer-discover-feed-recommendations)

## Staff UX Quantitative Researcher, AI Enablement

**Sunnyvale, California, or Seattle, Washington · Google experience level: Advanced**

Design measurements and analyse system logs to understand the performance and usability of AI infrastructure. The role combines quantitative research with programming and collaboration across engineering, product and data-science teams.

- **Minimum background:** a bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience; eight years in applied research or a similar setting; programming for data analysis; quantitative measurement and log analysis; and research involving AI/ML products, developer APIs or technical infrastructure.
- **Preferred areas:** relevant postgraduate research, substantial UX research experience and work with senior leadership.
- **Advertised US pay range:** USD 188,000–274,000, plus a 20% bonus target, equity and benefits under the employer's terms.

[Read the official advert and apply →](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/106568047426183878-staff-ux-quantitative-researcher-ai-enablement)
