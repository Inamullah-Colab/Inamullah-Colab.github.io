Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters are delivered by groups of universities. Applicants from around the world can apply, and some programmes offer full scholarships to highly ranked candidates. Applications go directly to the institution running the chosen programme.

## Make a shortlist you can act on

Start with the [official Erasmus Mundus information and catalogue](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/individuals/students/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters). Choose programmes that fit your previous studies and the skills you want to develop.

For each one, record:

- The exact programme and official application link.
- Academic and language requirements.
- Scholarship coverage and any costs you would still meet.
- The scholarship deadline, including its time zone.
- Required documents and referee arrangements.

## Check the programme’s own instructions

There is no single deadline that applies to every programme. The European Commission says applications commonly fall between October and January for the following academic year, but each programme publishes its own requirements.

Keep your shortlist small enough to prepare carefully tailored applications. Save a copy of the official call so you can check each requirement before submission.

**Source:** [European Commission — Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/individuals/students/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters), checked 8 November 2026.

*This is an application guide, not a notice that every programme is currently open.*
