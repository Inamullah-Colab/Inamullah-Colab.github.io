These Microsoft Cloud Operations & Innovation roles focus on the equipment and IT support behind cloud infrastructure. Both accept school-level qualifications alongside relevant practical experience; a university degree is not listed as a minimum requirement.

## Senior Data Center IT Support Technician

**South Paris, France · Job 200059317 · Posted 10 November 2026**

Provide support for workstations, laptops, servers and meeting-room audiovisual equipment. Tasks include maintaining spare-device inventories and keeping devices and operating systems updated.

- **Minimum requirements:** a high-school diploma or equivalent and at least three years supporting IT equipment or related technology.
- **Preferred background:** relevant technical study or equivalent work experience, applicable IT certifications and a driving licence.
- **Work arrangement:** fully on-site; travel below 25%.
- **Advertised annual French base pay:** €46,900–€68,200. Other compensation may apply.
- **Screening:** Microsoft Cloud background checks apply on joining or transfer and every two years afterwards.

## Data Center Technicians

**Jakarta, Indonesia · Job 200060870 · Posted 10 November 2026**

Prepare and install hardware, investigate equipment faults and remove or replace equipment following operational procedures. The position offers practical experience across data-centre deployment and maintenance.

- **Minimum requirements:** a high-school diploma, GED or equivalent; basic computer-hardware knowledge; and at least one year supporting IT equipment or related technology.
- **Schedule:** applicants must be able to work shifts, potentially including nights, weekends and public holidays.
- **Work arrangement:** fully on-site; travel below 25%.
- **Preferred background:** two or more years of relevant support work, server/network/storage troubleshooting and applicable technical certifications.
- **Salary:** no range stated in the advert checked.
- **Screening:** Microsoft Cloud background checks apply on joining or transfer and every two years afterwards.
