DAAD offers several scholarship programmes, rather than one award with a universal application process. The individual call is the source for eligibility, benefits, selection criteria and deadlines.

## Read the call before writing your statement

Open the [DAAD scholarship overview](https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/scholarships/daad-scholarships/) and follow its scholarship database link. Find the programme relevant to your study or research plans.

Use a simple two-column checklist: **requirement** and **evidence I can provide**. This makes gaps visible before you invest time in a long statement.

## Keep these questions beside your application

1. Does my qualification and background meet this call’s eligibility rules?
2. Which study programme or research project am I proposing?
3. What documents and references must arrive by the deadline?
4. Which costs are covered, and which are my responsibility?
5. Does admission require a separate university application?

Avoid borrowing the deadline or funding amount from a different DAAD programme. Follow the application portal and instructions linked from your selected call.

**Source:** [DAAD — Scholarships: An Overview](https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/scholarships/daad-scholarships/), checked 8 November 2026.

*Check the current call before applying. This guide does not promise eligibility or selection.*
