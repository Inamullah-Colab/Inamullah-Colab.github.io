Technology organisations also recruit people who manage programmes, explain infrastructure and build customer relationships. This selection includes three such roles, each with an Apply option on its official Google Careers advert when checked.

**Dates and availability:** the adverts reviewed did not show publication dates or fixed application deadlines. The date above is our verification date. Refer to the live adverts for availability and application conditions.

## Program Manager, Commercial Governance, Analytics and Invoicing

**Texas, USA · Remote eligible in Texas · Experience level: Mid**

Support the commercial and financial side of data-centre delivery. The work includes cost analysis, rate comparisons, project controls and improving invoice-review processes for West Texas operations.

- **Minimum background:** a relevant bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience; two years in programme or commercial management, financial operations or data analytics; and experience with financial controls and invoicing.
- **Preferred areas:** infrastructure or construction projects, dashboards, cost analysis and process improvement.
- **Advertised US pay range:** USD 132,000–189,000, plus a 15% bonus target, equity and benefits under the employer's terms.
- **Location limit:** the remote location listed is Texas; this does not establish permission to work from another country or state.

[Read the official advert and apply →](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/78528576772547270-program-manager-commercial-governance-analytics-and-invoicing)

## Communications Manager, Technical Infrastructure, EMEA

**London, UK; Helsinki, Finland; or Stockholm, Sweden · Experience level: Mid**

Develop communications about Google's technical infrastructure, including data centres and subsea cables. Responsibilities span media relationships, written materials, campaigns and advising teams on communications issues.

- **Minimum background:** a bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience; seven years in public and media relations involving technology or infrastructure; and work across teams.
- **Preferred areas:** cloud or enterprise technology, media relationships, project management and clear communication of technical subjects.
- **Advertised Swedish pay range:** SEK 960,000–980,000, plus a 15% bonus target, equity and benefits under the employer's terms. This range applies to Sweden; UK and Finnish ranges were not shown in the advert reviewed.

[Read the official advert and apply →](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/126055959020610246-communications-manager-technical-infrastructure-emea)

## Business Development Manager, New Business Sales, Export (Mandarin, English)

**Shanghai or Beijing, China · Experience level: Early**

Identify and develop new advertising customers, manage the sales process and recommend Google advertising products in response to clients' business goals.

- **Minimum background:** a bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience; two years of sales experience covering the full sales cycle; and fluent Mandarin and English for client communication.
- **Preferred areas:** export or e-commerce marketing, business expansion and a record of achieving sales goals.
- **Salary:** no range stated in the advert reviewed.
- **Experience note:** Google's “Early” label still includes a two-year experience requirement for this role.

[Read the official advert and apply →](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/92363761649623750-business-development-manager-new-business-sales-export-mandarin-english)
