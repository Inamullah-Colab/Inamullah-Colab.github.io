## Customer Engineer III, Platform, South, Google Cloud

**Office options: Austin, Atlanta, Addison, Houston, Raleigh or Durham, USA · Remote option: North Carolina, USA · Experience level: Advanced**

Work with sales teams and customers to design cloud solutions, demonstrate them through prototypes and workshops, and plan their implementation. The role combines architecture, troubleshooting and technical communication.

- **Minimum background:** a bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience; ten years in customer-facing or support work involving cloud-native architecture; relevant cloud, virtualisation or container experience; and technical presentations, programming, system design or demonstrations.
- **Location limit:** the advertised remote option is North Carolina; it is not a worldwide remote role.
- **Advertised US pay range:** USD 152,000–221,000, plus a 42.86% bonus target, equity and benefits under the employer's terms.

[Read the official advert and apply →](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/73091418758750918-customer-engineer-iii-platform-south-google-cloud)

## Electrical Manufacturing Engineer III, PIE AOE

**Sunnyvale, California, USA · Experience level: Mid**

Develop and qualify manufacturing processes for Google Cloud hardware. The role connects product design, printed-circuit-board assembly and manufacturing partners as new technologies move towards production.

- **Minimum background:** a relevant engineering bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience; two years in semiconductor-packaging or surface-mount manufacturing process development; PCBA process development; and experience introducing or qualifying production sites, lines or equipment.
- **Preferred areas:** relevant postgraduate study, manufacturing data systems, supplier collaboration and systematic process improvement.
- **Advertised US pay range:** USD 132,000–189,000, plus a 15% bonus target, equity and benefits under the employer's terms.

[Read the official advert and apply →](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/122071200032531142-electrical-manufacturing-engineer-iii-pie-aoe)

## Staff Mechanical Engineer, Debug

**Singapore · Experience level: Advanced**

Design and improve automated equipment with a team of mosquito biologists, software engineers and automation specialists. The advertised project develops technology intended to reduce the impact of mosquito-borne diseases, bringing engineering and biological research into the same product-development work.

- **Minimum background:** a mechanical-engineering or related bachelor's degree, or equivalent practical experience; eight years designing and testing electromechanical, pneumatic and fluid systems; prototyping; industrial or laboratory automation; and CAD modelling.
- **Work-authorisation note:** Google explicitly prioritises applicants who already have the right to work in Singapore without employer visa sponsorship.
- **Salary:** no range stated in the advert reviewed.
